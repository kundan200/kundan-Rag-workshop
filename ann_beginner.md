
# Artificial Neural Networks (ANN)

## Introduction
An Artificial Neural Network (ANN) is a machine learning
model inspired by the human brain. It consists of connected
artificial neurons that process information.

## Main Layers
An ANN typically contains three types of layers:

1. Input Layer: Receives the input data.
2. Hidden Layer: Processes information using weights,
   biases, and activation functions.
3. Output Layer: Produces the final prediction.

## Activation Functions
Activation functions introduce non-linearity into
neural networks. Common examples include ReLU,
Sigmoid, and Tanh.

## Training
Neural networks learn by adjusting their weights
and biases during training. Backpropagation
calculates gradients, and optimization algorithms
such as gradient descent update the parameters.

## Applications
Artificial Neural Networks are commonly used in
image recognition, speech recognition, medical
diagnosis, and predictive analytics.
